## 7. Limitations and Threats to Validity

### 7.1 Synthetic Fixture Limitations
- 100% attack traffic — cannot evaluate benign detection specificity
- Distribution aligned with training data — inflated accuracy expected
- Single attack type — does not test multi-attack generalization
- 31 windows (~31 minutes) — insufficient for temporal pattern diversity

### 7.2 Real Benchmark Limitations
- Raw files not downloaded locally due to license requirements and storage constraints
- Once downloaded, `scripts/evaluate_benchmark.py --all` will automatically process them
- Real results may differ significantly from synthetic fixture results

### 7.3 Temporal Forecasting Limitations
- Lead time metrics require benign→attack transitions
- Current fixture has no pre-attack benign baseline
- Forecasting evaluation requires multi-session datasets (CICIDS2017 ideal)

### 7.4 Model Age
- XGBoost trained on Phase 3 synthetic data (same distribution as sample)
- No real-world concept drift exposure
- Domain adaptation required before production deployment
